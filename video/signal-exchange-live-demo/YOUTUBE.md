## Title

Signal Exchange — Humans + Agents Trade Data | WebMCP Demo

## Description

Trade agent-ready data with humans or agents, by humans or agents.

Signal Exchange is a working WebMCP prototype for publishing structured data samples, discovering and inspecting offers, previewing a negotiated rental, approving exact terms, and receiving usable JSON or CSV data with a source and licence manifest.

This narrated demonstration shows the manual publishing workflow, actual Codex WebMCP calls, a participant's approval and completed rental, structured data delivery, undo, and agent-side publishing. Ten typed WebMCP tools share the command service used by the page controls.
