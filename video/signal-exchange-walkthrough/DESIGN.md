# Signal Exchange Video Design

## Overview

Signal Exchange is a full-screen premium data marketplace with an editorial market-table composition and a narrow approval desk. The visual language combines institutional trust with machine precision: warm serif statements sit over a dark technical grid, while brass structure, cyan data marks, and green verification communicate state. The video should feel like a real product demonstration, not a speculative brand film.

## Colors

- **Canvas**: `#040A10` — primary full-frame midnight background.
- **Header**: `#061019` — structured top bar and deep panels.
- **Raised Surface**: `#10202A` — transaction cards and code surfaces.
- **Primary Text**: `#F0E7D5` — warm high-contrast headlines.
- **Muted Text**: `#A99F91` — supporting copy and metadata.
- **Brass**: `#C99748` — rules, recommendation, and market value.
- **Bright Brass**: `#EFC46D` — focal highlights and CTA borders.
- **Machine Cyan**: `#6BC8E8` — WebMCP, schema, and signal marks.
- **Verified Green**: `#65D49A` — ready, verified, and access states.
- **Error Coral**: `#E8756D` — rejected/replay states only.

## Typography

- **Editorial voice**: Georgia, 700 for major claims and offer names; 60–104px in video.
- **Interface voice**: Arial, 400/700 for descriptions and controls; 22–34px.
- **Data voice**: Courier New, 700 for tool names, tokens, schemas, and tabular values; 18–28px.
- Use tabular numbers for wallet, price, trust, and duration.
- Georgia is the human decision register; Courier New is the agent/tool register. Their contrast is intentional.

## Elevation

Depth comes from nested one-pixel brass/cyan borders, hard-edged panel offsets, localized radial glows, and restrained perspective. Avoid generic soft cards and heavy drop shadows. The product screenshot sits inside a thin brass device frame and receives a slow camera push.

## Components

- **Market Table** — three full-width offer rows with identity, trust, freshness, formats, and credits.
- **Agent Pick Rail** — narrow brass recommendation strip aligned to the selected offer.
- **Activity Chronicle** — attributed WebMCP actions connected by a vertical timeline.
- **Goal & Budget Ledger** — fixed human constraints rendered as concise rows.
- **Deal Approval Desk** — bright brass proposal panel with exact before/after consequences.
- **Unlocked Schema Panel** — cyan code field list proving access state.
- **Compass Signal Mark** — circular brass brand mark used in opening and closing.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use literal palette values above and hard structural rules.
- Keep the product screenshot large and legible, with slow perspective drift.
- Animate data along visible paths and attribute every agent action.
- Let the 22-credit counteroffer and 78-credit wallet become the visual climax.
- Use brass for value/consent and cyan for machine-readable capability.

### Don'ts

- Do not use neon rainbow gradients, glass pills, or generic AI particles.
- Do not make the transaction look like real money or real seller communication.
- Do not invent additional tools, offers, metrics, or capabilities.
- Do not shrink body text below 20px or data labels below 16px.
- Do not hide the human approval boundary behind decorative motion.
